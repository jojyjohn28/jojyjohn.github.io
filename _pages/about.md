I am a researcher in **microbial ecology and ecophysiology**, studying how microbial communities respond, adapt, and recover under environmental stress and extreme conditions.

My work integrates **metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, genome-resolved analysis, whole-genome sequencing, and statistical modeling** to link microbial diversity with ecosystem function.

Currently affiliated with the **Department of Biological Sciences, Clemson University**, my research spans marine, estuarine, and soil ecosystems.
